. in classical snake game, player controls a snake in a playing field, collecting orbs which give the player points and make the snake grow in size, while avoiding the walls and the snake's own longer body. 1. Initial Setup: Create a square grid of cells. Place the snake in the center of the grid with its head pointing initially to the right. Place food in a random empty spot on the grid. Set the initial length, speed, and score of the player. 2. Main Game Loop: As long as the game is not over, repeat the following sequence: Receive Input: Detect any directional keys pressed by the player (up, down, left, right) and change the snake's heading accordingly, while preventing illegal 180-degree turns. Move Snake: Advance the snake's head one cell in its current direction. Shift the rest of the snake's body forward, with the head at the front. If the food was eaten on the previous turn, do not remove the tail; otherwise, remove it. Check for Collision: If the new head position is on the edge of the grid, the game ends. If the new head position occupies the same cell as any other part of the snake's body, the game ends. Check for Food: If the new head position is on the cell containing food, the food disappears. The snake's length increases by one segment, and its score increases by one point. A new food item is placed in a random, unoccupied cell. The game speed is increased slightly to increase the challenge. 3. Game Over: Upon detecting a boundary or self-collision, the game terminates. Display the final score to the player.
